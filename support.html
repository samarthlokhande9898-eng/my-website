YOU DON'T HAVE TO FIGURE IT OUT ALONE

Support starts with connection.

Choose one small next step. You deserve to be heard and treated with respect.

01

Talk to someone you trust

A trusted family member, teacher, college counsellor, or another responsible adult can help you decide what to do next.

02

Reach a qualified professional

A doctor or mental-health professional can offer appropriate, individual guidance and explain available support options.

03

Support a friend kindly

Listen calmly, avoid labels, do not promise to keep a dangerous situation secret, and involve a trusted adult when safety is at risk.

Need urgent help?

This student project is not monitored and cannot provide emergency support. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or a trusted adult right away.
